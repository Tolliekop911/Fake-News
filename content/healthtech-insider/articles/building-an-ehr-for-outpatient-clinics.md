Building an EHR for outpatient clinics is deceptively hard. The user-facing product is only part of the work; compliance, data handling and distribution are where many health-tech startups stall.

## The product surface

Clinicians judge an EHR on everyday friction: how fast a note gets written, how few clicks a task takes. Winning teams obsess over workflow detail.

## The invisible work

Security, data protection, uptime and audit trails are non-negotiable in healthcare and consume enormous engineering effort that users never see directly.

## Distribution

Clinics are cautious buyers. Trust, references and a clear migration path matter as much as features.
