AI documentation tools are easy to demo and hard to evaluate. A good demo tells you little about how a tool performs on your real inputs, over time, under review.

## Our evaluation methodology

When we evaluate a tool, we look at:

1. **Accuracy on representative inputs**, not cherry-picked examples.
2. **Review cost** — how long it takes a human to check and fix a draft.
3. **Privacy and data handling** — where data goes and how it is stored.
4. **Failure behavior** — what happens when the tool is wrong, and whether errors are easy to catch.

## Why human review is the real metric

A tool that produces a fast draft but requires heavy correction may save less time than it appears. Measure the full loop.

## Qualify every claim

We do not report accuracy or time-savings numbers unless the vendor documents them or we can measure them. We do not claim one tool is superior to another without evidence.
