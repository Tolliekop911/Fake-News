Revenue-cycle management (RCM) is the full journey of a dollar from the moment a patient books a visit to the moment the clinic is paid. Understanding each stage is the first step to reducing leakage.

## The stages

1. **Eligibility and authorization** — confirming coverage before care.
2. **Documentation and coding** — turning the visit into billable codes.
3. **Claim submission** — sending the claim to the payer.
4. **Denials and appeals** — handling what comes back unpaid.
5. **Patient balances** — collecting the remainder.

## Where clinics lose money

The most common leaks are expired authorizations, coding errors and unworked denials. Each is measurable and addressable.

## What to track

Days in A/R, denial rate and clean-claim rate are the core metrics. Software can surface them, but someone has to act on them.
