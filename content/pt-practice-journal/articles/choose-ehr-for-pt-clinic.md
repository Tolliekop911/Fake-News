Physical therapy practices have documentation, authorization and billing needs that general-purpose EHRs often handle poorly. Choosing software built for outpatient rehab can save hours a week — but only if you evaluate it against your real workflows.

## 1. Documentation built for rehab

Look for note templates and flows designed for PT evaluations, daily notes and progress notes — not adapted from primary care.

## 2. Authorization tracking

Outpatient PT lives and dies by visit authorizations. The software should track authorized visits, warn before they run out, and make re-authorization straightforward.

## 3. Scheduling and intake

Recurring appointments, cancellations and digital intake all affect throughput. Evaluate how the system handles them.

## 4. Billing and revenue cycle

Understand how charges flow from a finished note to a claim, and what reporting you get.

## 5. Migration and pricing

Ask exactly how your current data moves over, and get pricing in writing with everything that costs extra spelled out.
