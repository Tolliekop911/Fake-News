Choosing an electronic health record system is one of the highest-stakes software decisions an outpatient practice makes. The right framework matters more than any single feature list.

## Start with workflow, not features

The best-fitting EHR is the one that matches how your clinicians actually work. Map your documentation, intake, scheduling and billing workflows first, then evaluate systems against them.

## Evaluate these dimensions

1. **Documentation time** — how long does a typical note take, start to finish?
2. **Interoperability** — can it exchange records with the systems you depend on?
3. **Pricing transparency** — what is included, and what costs extra?
4. **Migration** — how does data move from your current system?
5. **Support and training** — what does onboarding look like?

## Verify every claim

Ask vendors for evidence behind performance claims, and talk to current customers where possible.
