# Compliance Checklist (Legal, Privacy & Search Policy)

**This is not legal advice and the system is not legally certified.** Verify every item
against the current official rules and have qualified counsel review before launch. Items are
grouped as **Legal requirement**, **Platform policy**, or **Recommended practice** so the
distinction stays clear.

## 1. Ownership transparency (Legal + Platform)
- The network is owned by Cowboy Systems. This is disclosed on every page, in the footer,
  and in JSON-LD (`parentOrganization`). **Do not** claim editorial independence from the owner.
- Google's **site-reputation-abuse** and spam policies: verify the current Search Essentials
  and spam policies. Do not use the network to manufacture the appearance of independent
  third-party authority, and do not conceal ownership.

## 2. Advertising & sponsored content (Legal — US FTC + Platform)
- **FTC** endorsement/disclosure rules: sponsored or company-controlled content must be
  clearly and conspicuously disclosed (the platform labels it and adds a disclosure box).
- Do not present company-written comparisons as independent reviews; state methodology and
  the commercial relationship.
- **South African** advertising rules (ARB code) and consumer-protection law also apply to
  the operating entity — confirm with counsel which regimes bind you.

## 3. Defamation & misleading representations (Legal — US + SA)
- No fabricated negative stories about competitors; no unverified claims about real people or
  companies. SA Consumer Protection Act and US state UDAP laws both reach misleading claims.

## 4. Healthcare advertising & medical claims (Legal)
- No guarantees of reimbursement, revenue, savings, claim acceptance, or clinical outcomes.
- No personalized medical or investment advice.
- Review FDA/FTC health-claim guidance for any statement that could read as a medical claim.

## 5. Privacy & data protection (Legal)
- **POPIA** (South Africa), **US state privacy laws** (e.g. CCPA/CPRA) and **GDPR** (if EU
  visitors) may all apply depending on operations and audience. Confirm applicability.
- Consent banner must default to the privacy-preserving option; load analytics/marketing
  cookies only after consent. Do not put personal data in URL parameters.
- Publish a counsel-reviewed privacy policy and honor data-subject requests.

## 6. Email marketing (Legal)
- **US CAN-SPAM** and, where applicable, POPIA/GDPR consent rules: honest headers, physical
  address, working unsubscribe, consent where required.

## 7. Copyright & image licensing (Legal)
- Use only licensed/owned images with proper attribution; store credits (the article schema
  has `imageCredit`). Do not copy other publishers' logos, trademarks or distinctive designs.
- Reproduce third-party text only within fair-use limits with attribution.

## 8. Google Search & News policies (Platform)
- **Scaled-content abuse:** do not mass-produce low-value articles. This platform ships 100
  *briefs* and a few vetted sample articles by design, not 100 auto-published articles.
- **Doorway abuse:** do not create near-duplicate pages across the five domains to occupy more
  results. The keyword tracker flags intra-publication cannibalization; also avoid forcing the
  same keyword across all five domains.
- **Link spam / fabricated backlinks / cloaking / hidden text / deceptive redirects:** none are
  implemented and none should be added.
- **Google News** (if applying): review its content and eligibility policies separately;
  transparent ownership and author/date accuracy are prerequisites.
